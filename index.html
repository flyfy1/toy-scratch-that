🎟️ Scratch That: Scratch the silver off a card. Win a nap.

A paper scratch card and a coin. Rub the silver off in ragged streaks, push the shavings around, and find out what you won: usually five paid minutes of staring at the wall. No money, no sign-up, and you can write a card for a friend.

How to play

  1. Press and rub anywhere on the card with a finger or the mouse. A brass token scrapes the silver off in ragged streaks; the first pass leaves flecks behind and a second pass wipes them clean. Shavings fly off and slide off the card.
  2. Find three matching pictures in the big panel. Three alike is a win and gets circled in red marker; one short gets a rubber stamp. Then scratch the prize strip to see what you won, for example "5 paid minutes of staring at the wall".
  3. The strip marked "Do not scratch here" can be scratched. The card will tell you off.
  4. Press "Another one" for the next card. Your cards and wins are counted on your device only.
  5. Type a prize in the box at the top, for example "I owe you a bubble tea". It goes into the page link (?w=...). A friend who opens the link gets a card that always wins your prize.

FAQ

Is there a scratch card I can scratch online for free?

Yes. Scratch That at scratch.integ.life is a free browser toy. Rub the silver panel with your finger or mouse and it scrapes off the way a real card does, with shavings and sound. Nothing to install and no account.

Can I win money?

No. It is not a lottery and no money is involved in any direction. Prizes are jokes, such as "5 paid minutes of staring at the wall" or "one nap, do not get caught".

Can I make a scratch card for a friend?

Yes. Type a prize in the box at the top, for example "I owe you a bubble tea". The prize is stored in the page link. Send the link and your friend gets a card that always wins your prize.

What happens if I scratch the "do not scratch here" strip?

The card tells you off. Nothing else happens; your prize still counts.

Is Scratch That free?

Yes. It is free, with no sign-up, no download and no ads. It runs in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers.

Does it have sound and vibration?

Yes. The scraping is synthesised live in the browser with the Web Audio API and follows the speed of your finger; it sounds different on foil and on bare paper. There are no audio files to load. On Android phones you also feel a light buzz while there is foil under the coin; iPhone browsers do not support web vibration.

Which languages does it support?

English, 简体中文 (Simplified Chinese), Español, Français and 日本語 (Japanese). The language is picked automatically from your browser settings; any other language falls back to English.
